Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Sketch VR lets you overlay a sketch image on your camera view in the browser, and pin it to a wall or table in a Meta Quest headset. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Who runs this app

Sketch VR is made by Tannaz Mohamadi Akbari. For privacy questions or requests, contact tannaz.amst@gmail.com.

What the app uses

  • Camera: the live camera view (in the browser) or the headset's passthrough view (in the headset) is shown on your screen so you can trace over it. The camera is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere by the app.
  • Sketch image: the image you choose is loaded into the app on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Room surfaces (headset only): in the headset the app uses the walls and tables it detects, plus positions it saves for the sketch, so the sketch stays where you pinned it. This is used live while the app runs. The app does not save or send your room layout.
  • Controller and headset position: used live to aim, pin and move the sketch. Not saved or sent.

What the app stores

Sketch VR does not have accounts and does not store your data on any server of ours. The app does save the files it needs to work offline in your browser's cache (a service worker). You can remove these at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser or by removing the app.

Third parties

  • Three.js (3D library): loaded from the jsDelivr content network. jsDelivr receives standard request information (such as your IP address) when the file is downloaded. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: the app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under GitHub's privacy statement.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No recording of the camera, the headset view, or your drawings.

Your choices

  • You can refuse camera access. The camera view then won't start, but you can still use the app.
  • You can choose not to load a sketch image at all.
  • You can clear the app's saved files by clearing the site data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

Deleting your data

The app keeps nothing on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything stored on your device, clear the site data in your browser or uninstall the app. If you have a question about your data, email tannaz.amst@gmail.com.

Children

Sketch VR does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
